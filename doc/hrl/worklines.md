### WP0 decisions taken 2026-08-27

1. **Upper body runs FREE.** Hardware-observed: freeing it markedly reduces the
   jitter/twitchiness. This retires the `hold_joint_ids` question that ADR-0005 step 3b
   left open, and it removes the sim/deploy interface mismatch the plan flagged (up to 42
   of 94 HL obs dims inconsistent under the hold). All four arms train and deploy free.
   ⚠ Consequence for the record: the 225.8 s A0 hardware baseline (2026-07-23) was taken
   **held**, so it is not a like-for-like control for anything measured free.
2. **Zero-command sampling raised in training.** Fixes the standing-still behaviour, and
   is the leading explanation for the HL emitting a nonzero goal at zero command. Under
   uniform box sampling the near-zero ball is ~0.1% of the volume while a whole
   behavioural regime is gated at ‖c‖ ≤ 0.1. The plan's conditional zero-command goal gate
   is therefore **not** triggered so far; keep it unbuilt unless measurement says otherwise.
   Relates to the lateral phantom (`|g|vy`, not `|g|vx`).
3. **Ankle-limit safety trips: REOPENED, knowingly (ADR-0009, 2026-08-28).** ADR-0008 did
   drive the commanded excess down (over-limit rate 0.00193 → 0.00001, mean excess 0.008676 →
   0.000003 rad/step), and the nonzero residual did show the L1 penalty had shaped the policy
   rather than a clamp masking it. **That shaping is exactly the problem.** The same penalty
   pushed the ankle command ~1.8 action-sigma off the bound, costing **2.1-2.4x of the roll
   range** — measured in sim and on hardware, agreeing to a few percent — in the joint that
   rejects lateral pushes. Model v3 is reverted; A0 again commands past its stops, and the
   measured hardware consequence is nil because `State_RLBase.cpp:160-165` truncates
   unconditionally and always did. This is a fidelity gap, not a fault. `clip without penalty`
   is untested and stays open. Full record → `docs/adr/0009`.

## Standing rule for the planning chat (WL-A)

When this chat catches itself deep in implementation (multi-file edits, debugging
loops, running trainings), stop and emit a hand-off instead: that work is usually too
easy for the expensive model and burns the planning context. Small, objectively
verifiable one-offs may go to background subagents (sonnet) directly.
